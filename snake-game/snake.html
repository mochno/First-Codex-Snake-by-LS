HOW TO
  • Move with Arrow keys or W/A/S/D. Press F to shoot (after picking gun). Press Space to pause.
  • Eat apples to score, speed up, and grow by +10% every 15 apples.
  • Eat egg to spawn helper snakes and expand the map.
  • Helper snakes chase apples, speed up gameplay, and vanish with explosion after 10s.
  • Flower triggers triangle mode: 5s blinking prep, then 5s active triangle zone.
  • During active triangle phase, you must stay inside the triangle.
  • Gun enables mouth-shooting in movement direction; bullets can destroy helper snakes.
  • Ambulance slows snake movement when collected.
  • Flower and gun disappear if not collected, then reappear again.
