HOW TO
  • Move with Arrow keys or W/A/S/D. Press F to shoot after grabbing the gun, Shift to dash when dash power is active, and Space to pause.
  • Pick a mode: Classic keeps it cleaner, Chaos enables the full current rule set, and Survival is harsher but rewards staying alive.
  • Quick apple chains build combo score. Every 15 apples still grows the snake by +10%.
  • Eggs spawn helper snakes and expand the field. Shooting helpers now drops apples and bonus points.
  • Flowers warn before triangle mode. Surviving the triangle pays out each second and gives a completion bonus.
  • Gun enables mouth-shooting in movement direction.
  • Ambulance slows the snake. Power crates rotate between shield, magnet, dash, and slow-time effects.
  • Complete rotating mini-tasks for extra points on top of combo and wave rewards.
  • Named wave events now spike the pace with helper rushes, harvest rounds, and triangle trials.
